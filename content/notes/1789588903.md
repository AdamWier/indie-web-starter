Using the library is resistance. Resistance to the latest craze Netflix is pushing on you. Resistance to the idea that we can not share, we must rent from a private company. Resistance to a carefully crafted version of reality. Using the library, in an era where libraries are painted as relics, is taking back your attention, your cognition, your self. Go, get lost in the books and films and magazines and objects. You'll find yourself once again.
